# Market Research Framework

This framework provides a systematic approach for conducting market research and identifying high-potential product opportunities.

## 1. Competitor Identification
*   **Search Queries:** Use queries like "top [category] software 2026", "competitors to [leading product]", and "[category] market landscape".
*   **Categories:** Categorize competitors by:
    *   **Academic/Institutional** (e.g., Turnitin)
    *   **Enterprise/Global** (e.g., Copyleaks)
    *   **Content/SEO** (e.g., Originality.ai)
    *   **Consumer/Casual** (e.g., Grammarly)

## 2. Feature & Pricing Analysis
For each top competitor, identify:
*   **Core Value Prop:** What is their primary marketing hook?
*   **Key Features:** What unique technical capabilities do they offer?
*   **Pricing Models:** Subscription, pay-per-use, or institutional quotes?
*   **Target Audience:** Who are they specifically trying to serve?

## 3. Trend & Gap Detection
Look for shifts in technology or regulation:
*   **Technological Shifts:** (e.g., AI integration, multi-modal detection).
*   **Regulatory Changes:** (e.g., EU AI Act, C2PA standards).
*   **Market Gaps:** Where is the competition failing? Common gaps include:
    *   **Logic vs. Syntax:** Can they detect stolen ideas, or just stolen words?
    *   **Bias:** Do they unfairly flag specific demographics (e.g., ESL)?
    *   **Authorship Verification:** Do they prove *who* did the work, or just *what* was produced?

## 4. Opportunity Selection
Filter opportunities based on:
*   **Technical Feasibility:** Can this be built with current or near-term technology?
*   **Market Demand:** Is there a clear "pain point" that existing tools don't solve?
*   **Defensibility:** How hard is it for incumbents to copy this feature?

## 5. Report Structure
All reports should follow the standardized Markdown format defined in the `format_report.cjs` script.
